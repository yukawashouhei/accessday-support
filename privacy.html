Privacy Policy

Updated September 11, 2026. Access Day is provided by Shohei Yukawa.

Your plans stay on your device

Outing titles, dates, selected facilities, equipment choices, personal notes and your confirmation times are stored in the app’s local storage. The developer does not receive this content. Facility searches and filters run on the device. There is no account sign-up, advertising SDK, AI service, contact access or location permission. The app does not determine or transmit your current location.

Apple device backups may include local app data according to your settings. You can remove a card in its detail screen. Removing the app removes local storage. Manage device backups separately in Apple settings.

When information leaves the app

PDF exports contain the specific card you choose, including its selected facilities and your notes. The app opens the iOS sharing sheet and sends nothing automatically. Choose your recipient or destination carefully. Temporary PDF copies in the app’s temporary directory are cleared after 24 hours when you next export; copies you save or share are managed by their destination.

Updating facility data requests a public file from the Tokyo Metropolitan Government. No card contents or search terms are included in that request. The server receives normal connection information such as your IP address. The map uses Apple MapKit and requests map data for displayed areas. Apple handles network information associated with that service. Opening external links is subject to the destination’s policy.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat retrieves product information, validates and restores purchases, and determines access to the optional one-time upgrade. It processes transaction information, an anonymous app user identifier and technical information such as app, device and operating-system versions for purchase processing and purchase analytics. Your outing content is never added to purchase data. We do not connect the anonymous identifier with an account or personal profile, and do not use it for advertising tracking.

See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy. Deleting local app data does not erase payment records or refund purchases.

Support

If you email support, your email address and the details you choose to send are used to answer your request. Do not include private outing plans, health or care details. For support and privacy requests, contact tangocards.support@gmail.com. Material policy changes will appear here with an updated date.

日本語の説明

外出名・日付・施設候補・設備の選択・メモ・確認時刻は端末内に保存します。開発者に送信しません。検索や絞り込みも端末内で行います。アカウント登録、広告SDK、AIサービス、連絡先へのアクセス、位置情報の取得はありません。

PDFには、選んだカードの内容とメモが含まれます。iOSの共有画面で自分が選んだ保存先・共有先にだけ渡します。自動送信はしません。アプリ内の一時PDFは、作成から24時間経過後の次の書き出し時に削除します。共有・保存したコピーは保存先で管理してください。

公表データの更新時は東京都の公開ファイルを取得します。カード内容や検索語は送信しませんが、通信先にIPアドレス等の通常の通信情報が渡ります。地図の表示にはApple MapKitを使い、表示範囲の地図を要求します。現在地は取得・送信しません。外部リンク先では各サービスの方針が適用されます。

Appleが決済を行い、RevenueCatが商品情報の取得、購入確認、復元、追加機能の利用権限を管理します。取引情報・匿名のアプリ利用者ID・端末やOS等の技術情報を購入処理と購入履歴の分析に用います。外出の内容を購入情報に追加せず、匿名IDを個人のアカウントと結び付けたり広告追跡に使ったりしません。

カードは詳細画面で削除できます。アプリの削除は端末内の保存内容を消します。Appleの購入記録・端末のバックアップは別に管理されます。メールのお問い合わせでは、アドレスと送信された内容を回答に使用します。外出予定や健康・介助に関する個人情報を送る必要はありません。お問い合わせ先は上記のメールアドレスです。
